Embodied carbon of concrete – Market Benchmark

The 2026 update of the Low Carbon Concrete Group Market Benchmark

Figure 1: LCCG Market Benchmark for embodied carbon, normal weight concrete, LCA stages A1 to A3.

  • The benchmark ratings are based on embodied carbon of normal weight concrete mixes used recently in the UK.
  • Performance requirements may make it impractical to achieve some ratings for a particular application.
  • Opportunities for reducing the carbon rating may typically be achieved by adjusting: type and % of SCM, requirements for early strength gain, consistence, environment (e.g. by use of protective barrier layers), cement content (kg/m³), w/c ratio, use of admixtures, type and grading of aggregates, age at which the specified strength must be achieved, sources of constituents.

Volume-weighted embodied carbon

Totals and averages weighted by the volume of each mix, compared with UK-average concrete of the same strengths and volumes (the benchmark mean).

Distribution across ratings

Share of rated mixes in each rating. Each of LCCG-1 to LCCG-5 represents 20% of the market data (the markers).

Fractiles assume a normal distribution fitted to the values entered, as in the spreadsheet.

Distribution by strength class

Number of mixes in each rating for each strength class. Strengths between standard classes are rated by interpolation and counted on their own row.

Table 1: 2026 LCCG Market Update – Table of boundary values between the embodied carbon ratings, kgCO₂e/m³

A mix takes the rating whose lower boundary its embodied carbon meets or exceeds. Between standard classes, boundaries are interpolated linearly.

Ratings

A 1 to 5 rating system, each band representing 20% of the data collected and split into four subdivisions. Mixes rated 1.1 fall within the top performing 5% (lowest emissions); those rated 5.4 are in the bottom 5% (highest emissions).

Convert to characteristic cube strength

Cylinder strengths are converted with the smoothed cube-to-cylinder factor derived from BS EN 1992-1-1 Table 3.1, interpolated between strength classes. 1 MPa = 145.038 psi.

About the benchmark

The LCCG Market Benchmark summarises the distribution of cradle-to-gate carbon emissions of normal weight concrete recently produced in the UK. It covers LCA stages A1 to A3 (cradle to batching plant gate, or cradle to precasting mould) and is updated periodically, no more than once a year.

Market beating mixes have lower embodied carbon than the range of submitted market data; outlier mixes have higher. Classes C80/95 to C100/115 have insufficient data to be rated, and strengths below 8 MPa cube are outside the benchmark.

How to reference ratings

State the version of the benchmark used, for example “LCCG Benchmark 2026”. Where a rating is included in a project specification, it may be sensible to define the version that will be used to assess the placed concrete. It may be more appropriate to specify an average rating across all mixes and uses, volume weighted, than individual ratings for each mix or element.
